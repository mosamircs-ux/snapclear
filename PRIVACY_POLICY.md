# Privacy Policy for Lumina Saver

**Last Updated:** November 19, 2026

**Lumina Saver** ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed and published by Mohamed Samir Amin. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices concerning data collection, use, and disclosure when you use our mobile application.

---

### 1. 100% On-Device Processing
Lumina Saver is designed as an on-device utility:
- **No External Media Processing Servers:** Video and audio processing, conversion, and MP3 extraction are performed locally on your device using built-in engine components and FFmpeg.
- **Local Storage:** Any media files, audio files, or downloads saved using Lumina Saver are stored strictly in your device's local storage (`Downloads/LuminaSaver`). We do not host, store, or transmit your downloaded files to any external servers.

---

### 2. Information We Do Not Collect
- We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal identification information (such as name, email address, phone number, or physical address).
- We do not access, collect, or store your contacts, browsing history outside the app, or personal media files.

---

### 3. Advertising & Analytics (Google AdMob)
The App displays advertisements served by **Google AdMob** to support development and maintenance.

Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers and device data to serve relevant ads and prevent fraud:
- **Advertising ID (Google Play Services ID):** Used by Google to personalize or serve contextual advertisements in compliance with Google Play Developer policies.
- **Diagnostics and Performance Data:** Crash logs, app responsiveness, and ad interaction metrics collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.
- **User Consent:** For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, we implement the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to obtain user consent in accordance with GDPR regulations.

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

---

### 4. Required App Permissions
Lumina Saver requests only essential Android permissions:
- **Internet (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Required to fetch online media links you request and to load AdMob advertisements.
- **Network State (`android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`):** Used to check network connectivity before initiating download requests.
- **Foreground Service & Notifications:** Used to display ongoing download progress notifications in your status bar.

---

### 5. Third-Party Services & Links

### 6. Children's Privacy
Lumina Saver does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. The App is targeted at a general audience aged 18 and older.
